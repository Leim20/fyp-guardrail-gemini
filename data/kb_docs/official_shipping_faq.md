Standard delivery within Peninsular Malaysia takes 3 to 5 working days, and East Malaysia takes
5 to 8 working days. You can follow your parcel from the order page.

Tracking updates can lag behind the courier by up to 48 hours. If your parcel is more than
7 days late, ask the support assistant to open a ticket and we will arrange a redelivery or
investigate with the courier.
