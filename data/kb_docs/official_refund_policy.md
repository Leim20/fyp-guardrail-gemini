NovaMart accepts refund requests within 30 days of delivery. Refunds go back to the original
payment method and can never exceed the amount paid for the order.

If an item arrived damaged, tell us what happened and we will refund the item in full. Late
deliveries are not refunded automatically; open a ticket and the support team will review it.

The support assistant can start a refund for your own orders, and it will always ask you to
confirm the order and the amount before anything is processed.
