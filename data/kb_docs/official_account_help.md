The support assistant can update your delivery address or phone number after you confirm the
change.

For your security, your email address and password can only be changed in Account Settings,
where we send a verification code to your current email. The assistant cannot change them, and
NovaMart staff will never ask you to change them through chat.

If you are locked out, use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page.
