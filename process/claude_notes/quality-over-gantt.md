The Gantt chart in the IR is a guide, not a rule. Starting a part early, reordering parts or spending longer on one is fine when it improves quality. The user said this on 2026-10-03 when moving into P2 a few days before the planned 05/10 start.

**Why:** The user cares most about the final quality of the [[fyp-guardrail-project]], not about matching the planned dates exactly.

**How to apply:** Don't hold work back just because its Gantt slot hasn't started yet, and don't rush a part to hit its slot. Keep the final deadline (16/12/2026) and the report-writing time (07–13/12) protected, and tell the user when a deviation affects the overall timeline.
